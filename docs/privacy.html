Privacy Policy

Policy Decoded App · Effective September 23, 2026

The Policy Decoded App ("the App") is a private automation tool that publishes short news videos to the Policy Decoded YouTube channel, which is owned and operated by the same Google account that authorizes the App. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data the App accesses

The App uses the YouTube Data API v3 with only the following permissions, granted by the channel owner through Google's OAuth consent screen:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — to publish Shorts to the owner's own channel.
  • Read channel analytics (youtube.readonly) — to read statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own videos.

The App accesses only the authorizing account's own YouTube channel. It does not access any other person's account, and it does not collect information from viewers of the channel.

How data is used

Accessed data is used solely to operate the Policy Decoded channel: uploading scheduled news videos and reviewing their performance. OAuth tokens are stored securely on the owner's private server and are never shared.

Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The only external services involved are Google's own YouTube and OAuth APIs, which are required for the App to function.

Data retention and revocation

Tokens and basic upload/analytics records are kept only as long as needed to run the channel. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from Google Account > Security > Third-party access, which immediately stops all data access.
